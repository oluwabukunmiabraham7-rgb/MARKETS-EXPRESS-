Privacy policy

Last updated: October 27, 2026

MARKET EXPRESS uses the information you provide to support account access, process order requests and help you check order status.

Information we handle

Depending on how you use the website, this can include your name, email address, phone number, delivery address, order items and order status.

Storage and security

Authentication and order information are handled through Firebase services. Access to customer orders should be restricted by the Firestore security rules supplied with this project. Do not deploy until those rules are applied and tested.

Sharing

Order details may need to be used by the store to fulfil your request. Do not submit information that is not necessary for an order.

Your choices

You can choose not to create an account or place an order. Contact the store through its official channel to ask about your information. Add a verified contact channel before publishing this policy.

Payments and cookies

This website does not process online payments in this version. No advertising or analytics integrations are intentionally included in this source code.

Contact

Before launch, publish the store's verified customer-support contact details here.
